## Tier definitions (docs/MASTER_SPEC.md)

- BIOLOGICAL: measured connectome anatomy / measured model dynamics (probes).
- EXPERIMENTAL: published primary experimental results.
- CONNECTOMIC: computed from the v783 connectome.
- MODELED: our model rule (documented, not claimed as biology).
- ENGINEERED: task/protocol machinery, labelled, never credited to the brain.
