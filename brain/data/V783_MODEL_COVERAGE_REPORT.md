**Dominant exclusions (determinable reasons):**

* **537 photoreceptors** (`sub_class=photo_receptor`, all afferent visual). Photoreceptors have no traced synapses inside the FAFB brain volume; they carry no connectivity rows and cannot participate in the Shiu LIF network. Their exclusion is expected and affects nothing.
* **4 descending neurons** (nerve CV): `720575940618863553` (DNge061, right); `720575940618548244` (DNge061, right); `720575940619434587` (DNge153, right); `720575940621294218` (DNge061, left). These are inside the broad `DN_ALL` aggregate but are **not** members of any motor readout population used by the closed loop (see §3).
* **45 antennal-nerve afferents** (31 `SA_DMT_DMetaN` mechanosensory-ascending + 5 `AN_GNG` local/ascending GNG types + others) and small numbers of optic-lobe intrinsic, endocrine (pars_lateralis), olfactory and unknown-sensory cells. These are peripheral/ascending cells that were not in the authors' completed roster at the pinned materialization; no per-cell reason is published in the model repository.

## 4. Verdict for Gate 4 v2

**Not blocking.** The 616 excluded neurons are (a) 537 photoreceptors with no brain synapses, (b) 4 cervical-nerve DNge cells outside all used readout populations, and (c) 75 peripheral/ascending/optic/endocrine cells outside all used pathways. No sensory, MB, dopamine, or motor population of the v2 assay loses a single neuron. The v1 finding that the anatomical sugar→PAM and LC9→KC routes are dynamically silent is therefore NOT a coverage artifact of the excluded cells.
